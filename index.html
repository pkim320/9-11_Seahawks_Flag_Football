Defense reminders

  • Leverage: Stay between your player and the end zone, a bit inside.
  • Zero contact: Hands off. Move your feet instead.
  • Track the belt: Watch the waist and the flags, not the head fakes.
  • Pull the flag: Chop your feet, reach with both hands, pull, hold it up high.
  • Talk: Call Ball! and Switch!

Year-round program

Ages 9-11. Bodyweight only. No maxing out, and mix in other sports.

The weekly shape3 training days of about 30 minutes (warm-up, speed and agility, strength, cool-down). 1-2 skill days of 15-20 minutes. At least 1 full rest day. Take an easy week after every 8-10 weeks.

Parent and player tips

  • Consistency beats length. A few short sessions each week win.
  • Water breaks between every drill.
  • Keep it fun. Turn drills into games and beat yesterday's score.
  • Stop at 15-20 minutes. End while they still want more.
  • Praise effort and good technique, not just results.
  • Sore or in pain? Stop and rest. If it continues, check with a doctor.
